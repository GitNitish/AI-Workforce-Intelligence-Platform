# WorkForceIQ

> AI-Powered Workforce Intelligence Platform

## Overview

WorkForceIQ is an enterprise-inspired workforce intelligence platform designed to help organizations make better resource allocation decisions using analytics, forecasting, and artificial intelligence.

The platform simulates real-world workforce planning challenges commonly faced by IT services and consulting organizations. It combines workforce analytics, resource allocation, business KPIs, and AI-powered recommendations into a single decision-support platform.

This project is being built as a production-style software application to demonstrate software engineering, backend development, business analytics, data engineering, and AI integration.

---

## Business Problem

Organizations often manage hundreds or thousands of employees across multiple projects.

Resource managers spend significant time answering questions such as:

- Who is available?
- Which employee best matches a project?
- Which skills are currently in shortage?
- What is the current utilization percentage?
- Which projects are at staffing risk?

These decisions are frequently spread across spreadsheets, emails, and multiple systems, making workforce planning slow and inefficient.

---

## Proposed Solution

WorkForceIQ centralizes workforce data into a single platform that provides:

- Intelligent resource allocation
- Workforce analytics dashboards
- AI-powered recommendations
- Forecasting of future resource demand
- Executive-level business insights
